# Wide glass tray and stable theme surfaces — beta 75

The manual-expansion lifetime and profile opacity policy below are superseded by [beta 76](X-12.40.1-glass-controls-and-profile-transparency.md), which also removes the original phone bar reservation and adds compose visibility and glass-opacity controls.

The device report confirms beta 74, four owned glass buttons and UIKit glass support. The supplied Apollo screenshots are the behavior reference: spread-out tabs in a wide tray, minimizing to the selected tab at the leading edge and reopening on upward scroll or tap.

## Navigation

The phone tray now uses the available width with 20-point side margins. Its tabs are distributed across that width, with scrolling retained for unusually large custom tab sets. Scrolling down by 72 points minimizes it to a 58-point selected-tab button at the leading edge. Scrolling up by 12 points or tapping the button reveals the tray. Thresholds prevent jitter; a manual reveal stays open through the remaining scroll inertia. New pages/tabs, short pages and a return to the top reveal navigation. Right-to-left layout uses the opposite leading edge.

Keep tab bar visible and VoiceOver keep the tray expanded. The iPad rail stays expanded. Reduce Motion disables spring movement. Native tab callbacks, unread badges, reordering and long presses remain unchanged. Deliberate native hides, including fullscreen media, remain respected. The original scroll fade is pinned while the owned dock handles its own minimization, so it cannot fade out the remaining button.

Native timeline scroll callbacks and the owned media gallery supply scroll events; there are no global UIScrollView hooks, delegate replacements or scroll timers. The glass effect is retained across temporary geometry changes and reused across theme refreshes, avoiding repeated effect construction during navigation.

## Media and theme repairs

Beta 74 colored direct/composable planes by class and geometry. This was insufficient: a full-size touch highlight is an overlay above content and can obscure media when given an opaque fill. Beta 75 removes that plane mutation entirely. Ordinary status views also retain their native transparent backing, preserving separately hosted inline video. Quote and focal-post roots retain theme support.

Known root setters receive the themed color before assignment; window attachment and view appearance apply initial colors. No post or profile background is repainted after every layout. Assignments are idempotent and disable implicit color animation. Original native colors, including nil, are retained for restoring the default theme. Header, summary, bio, action-row and profile content roots are included. The profile's segmented tab bar and its collection backing are resolved through verified getters/object ivars; media and avatar/banner trees are excluded.

## Validation

Native regressions exercise the production scroll state machine, wide/compact/RTL geometry, minimum hit areas, repeat/reorder/stale-account routing, manual reveal during inertia, pinning and rubber-band/jitter behavior. Production surface tests include a full-size transparent touch overlay above photo/player views, verifying unchanged overlay/media colors, geometry and stacking plus repeated-refresh stability and nil restoration. Existing session, filtering, sidebar and navigation tests remain enabled. Physical-device rendering and touch behavior still require validation; the PR records the full iOS build result.
